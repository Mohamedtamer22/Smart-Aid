### 1.2 International ISEF Category Mapping: Systems Software (SOFT)
- **Primary Category:** **Systems Software (SOFT)**
- **Applicable Subcategory:** **Algorithms / Mobile Computing / System Architecture**
- **Justification:** Smart Aid is fundamentally an engineering software system designed to optimize emergency resource routing, deterministic triage classification, and geospatial multi-criteria decision support. - **Alternative / Interdisciplinary Considerations:**

  - *Computational Biology and Bioinformatics (CBIO):* Not appropriate as it does not involve genomic or molecular data.
  - *Conclusion:* **Systems Software (SOFT)** is the correct and defensible category.

## 3. Display & Safety Regulations Review

The physical display board must comply with official ISEF and local fair safety standards:

1. **Patient Data Privacy & De-Identification:**
   - **Rule:** No identifiable patient information, personal medical history, or real emergency records may appear on the poster or screen demos.
   - **Compliance:** All names (`Demo Citizen`, `Hamo User`), addresses, and coordinates (`30.045°N, 31.236°E`) in screenshots and demonstration data are fictional and synthetic.
2. **Medical Device & Clinical Diagnosis Claims:**
   - **Rule:** Software cannot claim to perform autonomous medical diagnosis or clinical treatment without regulatory clearance.
   - **Compliance:** The system is explicitly labeled as **decision-support software**. Disclaimers are prominently displayed: *"Decision support only; not an autonomous diagnosis or replacement for municipal emergency services."*
3. **No Biological or Chemical Hazards:**
   - **Rule:** No medications, blood products, chemical reagents, or hypodermic equipment may be displayed at the booth.
   - **Compliance:** Smart Aid is purely a software system. The booth will feature only digital computing devices (laptop, mobile phone, tablet) and informational display boards.
4. **Display Dimensions:**
   - **Local Specified Layout:** $140\text{ cm (Width)} \times 120\text{ cm (Height)}$, partitioned as: Left Panel ($35\text{ cm}$), Center Panel ($70\text{ cm}$), Right Panel ($35\text{ cm}$).
   - **ISEF Maximum Dimensions:** Displays at the international fair must not exceed $76\text{ cm (Depth)} \times 122\text{ cm (Width)} \times 274\text{ cm (Height)}$. If advancing to the international level, the $140\text{ cm}$ local board must be adapted to fit the $122\text{ cm}$ maximum ISEF width limit.
